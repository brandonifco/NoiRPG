The Overpass — Paper Prototype Print Kit

Print: Letter, portrait, background graphics ON, margins default. ~12 pages. Cut along card borders.

Deal: Facilitator keeps the four statement decks and the damage reference. Player gets the verb reference, one build sheet (their choice, dealt blind), and receives evidence cards as doors open during board play. Suspect dossier cards sit face-up at the table edge with pencil-tick trackers.

Table Kit — References & Suspect Dossiers

PLAYER REFERENCEthe four verbs
LISTEN — free. Statement goes on the record (always pinnable). Suspect recovers a little composure.
PRESS — pick Insight / Intimidate / Fast Talk vs their Stonewall. Success: −5 composure. Special: −10 + reveals a TELL. Failure: they harden (+5).
PRESENT EVIDENCE — name the card and the contradiction. If it truly contradicts: it always lands (no roll) — roll only grades the reaction. If it doesn't: +5 to them, and they learn what you hold.
CHANGE TOPIC — costs 1 Patience. Resets press momentum.
Every turn ticks Patience. At 0 the room ends — lawyer, walkout, captain.
FACILITATOR REFERENCEdamage & flow
Press success −5 · Press special −10 + tell · Landed contradiction −10 · Alibi collapse −15 · Failed press +5 (repeat fail on same thread: +1 Patience cost) · Wrong evidence +5 + they learn it.
Thresholds unlock marked cards. Composure 0 = break — play the dossier card's break line exactly; breaking is not always winning.
Read statements in order; branch notes override. Insight specials mark the card SHAKY on the transcript. Track per session: could the player state each contradiction before presenting? After a failed roll, can they say what changed?

BREAK (0): grief, then fury. Nothing case-useful — she knows nothing. Play it in full. This deck teaches that breaking a suspect isn't winning; it's just breaking a person.
